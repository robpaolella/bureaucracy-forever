The deal

What we expect of a raider

None of this is unusual. All of it is enforced.

Both progression nightsTuesday and Wednesday, roughly 90% of the tier. Life happens — tell us before the day.
Read the fight firstStrategy goes up in Discord before the night. Come knowing your job, not asking what it is.
Your own consumablesFlasks, potions, world buffs where they matter. The guild bank covers repairs, not your flasks.
Voice and an addon packIn Discord for every pull. Boss timers and a logging client running — we review the logs, not our memories.
Take the callOne person calls during a pull. Disagree between pulls — we would rather hear it than lose the night.
Two weeks of trialFull loot rights from night one. We tell you where you stand at the end, either way.

Apply

Pick a path. The Raider form asks for logs and a spec; the Social form asks for almost nothing.

Any recent raid. We care more about deaths avoided than damage done.
Your local time: 6–9 PM, America/Los_Angeles.
You'll get a Discord DM when an officer picks it up.

What happens next

  1. Within a day — an officer claims it and reads your logs.
  2. A short chat in Discord, usually fifteen minutes.
  3. Two-week trial with full loot rights.
  4. A straight answer at the end of it.

Not raiding?

Social members get the Discord, alt runs and the calendar. No attendance, no trial, no loot rules to memorise.

Questions first?

Join the Discord and ask in #recruitment. Nobody will pressure you into applying.
