# Guild supplement to the shared design reviewer

Use with [Maestro's reviewer instructions](/git/maestro/skills/design-check/reviewer-prompt.md),
not as a standalone prompt. The shared prompt owns evidence/authority, the fixed
comparison checklist, one PASS or numbered FIXES verdict and the single re-check.
Add these guild-specific checks to that same verdict:

- Preserve the phone calendar list rather than squeezing the desktop month grid.
  Calendar list changes cover both member and officer views at both widths.
- Match DESIGN.md's typography roles, Ink surfaces, Sand emphasis, Teal interaction,
  borders, spacing, density and established component variants. Operational pages
  remain usable tools rather than marketing layouts.
- Apply PRODUCT.md's 44px targets (except availability paint cells) and 4.5:1 text
  contrast. Ask for measured/browser evidence; a screenshot alone cannot certify
  target dimensions, contrast, keyboard access or a full accessibility audit.
- Check guild/viewer-local time labels and appropriate member/officer content;
  no private officer information may appear to members or logged-out visitors.
- PRODUCT.md supplies approved facts even where old live copy awaits correction.
  Do not fabricate claims or restore placeholder policy from handover artboards.
- Apply AGENTS.md's Designs rules: an element shown in Robert's approved design has
  his go-ahead; otherwise a new kind of element needs explicit approval. Uncovered
  cases and ambiguous conflicts go through the conductor to Robert.
- The local `/dev/session` substitute is not proof of real Discord login/role sync.
  Use the supplied feature action/result evidence for persistence claims.
